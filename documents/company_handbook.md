# Nordvik Analytics — Employee Handbook (Internal)

Version 4.2 · Effective 1 March 2026 · Owner: People Operations

## 1. Working hours and location

Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 in the employee's local time zone.
Outside core hours, scheduling is flexible. Nordvik Analytics operates a
remote-first policy: office attendance is voluntary except for the two
company-wide onsite weeks held in April and October.

Employees who relocate to a different country must notify People Operations
at least 45 days in advance, because payroll and tax registration must be
updated before the move takes effect.

## 2. Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar
year, in addition to national public holidays. Leave accrues monthly at two
days per month and may be taken from the first day of employment.

A maximum of 5 unused leave days may be carried over into the following year.
Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Leave requests longer than 10
consecutive working days require approval from both the direct manager and
the department head.

## 3. Expense policy

All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred. Receipts are
mandatory for any amount above 15 EUR.

Standard limits per employee:

- Hotel accommodation: 180 EUR per night in European cities, 260 EUR per
  night in London, Zurich, and New York.
- Meals while travelling: 55 EUR per day.
- Home office equipment: a one-off budget of 1,200 EUR, refreshed every
  three years.
- Professional development: 2,000 EUR per person per year, covering courses,
  conferences, and certifications.

Client entertainment above 500 EUR requires written pre-approval from the
Finance Director. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## 4. Customer support service levels

Support tickets are triaged into three severity levels:

- Severity 1 (production outage): first response within 1 hour, 24/7.
- Severity 2 (degraded functionality): first response within 4 business hours.
- Severity 3 (question or feature request): first response within 2 business
  days.

Enterprise-tier customers are additionally entitled to a named technical
account manager and a quarterly service review. The contractual uptime
commitment for the Nordvik platform is 99.9% measured monthly, with service
credits issued automatically when the target is missed.

## 5. Information security

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every system that holds customer
data. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are managed through the
company password manager; sharing credentials over chat or email is
prohibited.

Customer data may only be processed in the EU-West and EU-North regions.
Production database access is granted read-only by default; write access
requires a time-boxed elevation approved by the on-call engineering lead and
expires automatically after 8 hours.

Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@nordvik.example
within 1 hour of discovery. The incident response team has 72 hours to notify
affected customers, in line with GDPR obligations.

## 6. Performance and progression

Performance reviews run twice a year, in June and December. Promotion
decisions are made only in the December cycle. Every employee has a
documented career ladder with six levels, from Associate (L1) to Principal
(L6).

Employees who refer a candidate that is hired and passes probation receive a
referral bonus of 1,500 EUR, paid in the month after the probation period
ends.
